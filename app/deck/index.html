How's everything so far?

A serving robot stops at your table, sees that your drink is low, asks if you would like a refill, and acts on your answer — every model on the board.

No cloud call in the loop. The camera, the microphone, the models and the microcontroller are all on one device.

Why on the device

Tables are private

Cameras and microphones sit where people eat and talk. Nothing from this table leaves the board.

A venue is not a datacentre

Conference Wi-Fi drops. A refill should not wait on a round trip to a region on another coast.

Confidence is a decision

Reaching for a glass you only think is empty is worse than asking. A low score makes the robot ask rather than act.

Every service is a user systemd unit: enabled, restarted on failure, and back after a reboot without anyone logging in.

How it fits together

One Linux process owns the camera. The microcontroller owns the pins. Between them is a single command, not a video feed.

Is it really the NPU?

The graph is delegated

Each .pte contains a QNN delegate and no CPU kernels — not one aten:: operator left in the program.

The DSP is in use

During inference the vision and language processes hold /dev/fastrpc-cdsp handles: work is crossing to Hexagon.

What we do not claim

No CPU baseline was exported, so we do not quote a speed-up. Speech recognition runs on CPU and is labelled that way.

Choosing the speech model was a measurement, not a preference: the same twenty recordings went through both, so the pairs differ only by model. tiny.en matched base.en on 17 of 20, kept the drink name on 19 of 20, and cut transcription from 6.9s to 3.0s.

On a serving robot

  • It rides, it does not drive. Navigation stays with the robot; this module only looks at the table it stopped beside.
  • One board, one cable. Camera and microphone over USB, the display over Ethernet, everything else on the device.
  • The arm is the easy part to add. The microcontroller already runs a bounded sequence with a host watchdog; today it drives an LED instead of a gripper.
  • It degrades politely. If it mishears it asks again, and if it is unsure of the drink it asks which one rather than guessing.
