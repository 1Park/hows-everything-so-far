# Explicit dev fixtures

The bbox, drink, fill_ratio and confidence in vision.json are a **manual fixture** matched to an illustrative AI photo (docs/assets/cups-example.jpg). They are not real DINO/detection results. For the enough/uncertain scenarios, the service changes the fixture fill level/confidence to reproduce a skip. These bboxes do not match arbitrary uploaded images, and the UI indicates that it is a fixture.

drink_cases.json has 20 water/coke/coffee request-interpretation cases with expected intent/item. They are used to refine the prompt, so results on them should not be read as held-out accuracy. During evaluation, the real model raw output and the prompt are saved to new timestamped results. The fixed sentence in the default STT fixture is not a real transcription. Errors, timeouts and late results are injected and verified in tests/.

drink_cases_distorted.json contains **manually synthesized STT-like distorted sentences** that map by ID to the 20 cases above. They vary similar-sounding words, spelling, spacing and filler words, and inherit the current request and expected result from the originals. They are not real audio or measured STT output, and with heavy distortion even a person may find them ambiguous. `python scripts/evaluate_gemma.py` calls both sets with the same product prompt. Scores for the original and distorted sets are reported separately, and no claim is made about independent model generalization or real STT accuracy.

`python scripts/dev.py --mode fixture` combines Vision/STT/Hardware fixtures with real Qwen HTTP. To reproduce without any model, including Qwen, use `--mode offline`.

mock_events.jsonl is **mock history** for the dashboard (`scripts/make_mock_events.py`, regenerated with a fixed seed). It assumes an evening bar, 2026-10-11–17 16:00–23:00 (America/Los_Angeles, ts in UTC), with drinks coke/coffee/beer/water/cocktail. 88 detected lines (45 low, 43 high) and 37 requested lines about 30 seconds (20–45 seconds) after a low detection. Everything after 19:00 on demo day 10/17 is cut, so after that point real records are the most recent lines. The format is the same as `logs/events.jsonl`, and every line has `"mock": true`, so the lines stay distinguishable even when merged with real records. beer/cocktail are dashboard-only values that are not on the app menu (water/coke/coffee).
